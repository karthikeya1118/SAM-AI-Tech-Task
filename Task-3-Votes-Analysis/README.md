# Task 3 – Votes Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this task is to analyze restaurant votes and ratings to identify the restaurants with the highest and lowest number of votes and understand the relationship between votes and ratings.

## Dataset

The dataset contains restaurant information such as:

- Restaurant Name
- City
- Cuisines
- Rating
- Votes
- Price Range
- Online Delivery

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Restaurant with Highest Votes

The restaurant with the highest number of votes was identified using the Votes column.

### 2. Restaurant with Lowest Votes

The restaurant with the lowest number of votes was identified using the Votes column.

### 3. Votes vs Restaurant Rating

A scatter plot was created to visualize the relationship between the number of votes and restaurant ratings.

### 4. Correlation Analysis

The correlation between votes and ratings was calculated.

The correlation value obtained was:

**-0.09**

This indicates a very weak negative relationship between votes and ratings.

## Visualization

The following visualization was created:

- Votes vs Restaurant Ratings

## Key Insights

- The highest-voted restaurant represents the highest level of customer engagement in terms of votes.
- The lowest-voted restaurant has the least number of customer votes.
- The correlation value of **-0.09** indicates that there is very little relationship between the number of votes and restaurant ratings.
- A restaurant with more votes does not necessarily have a higher rating.

## Conclusion

The votes analysis shows that customer engagement and restaurant ratings are not strongly related in this dataset. With a correlation of **-0.09**, the relationship between votes and ratings is very weak. Therefore, a higher number of votes does not necessarily indicate a higher restaurant rating.
