# Task 2 – Data Visualization

## Objective

The objective of this task is to create meaningful visualizations from restaurant data and identify useful insights that can support business decision-making.

## Dataset

The dataset contains restaurant information such as:

- Restaurant Name
- City
- Cuisines
- Rating
- Votes
- Price Range
- Online Delivery

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Restaurant Ratings Distribution

A histogram was created to understand how restaurant ratings are distributed across the dataset.

### 2. Top 10 Cities by Number of Restaurants

A bar chart was created to identify the cities having the highest number of restaurants.

### 3. Top 10 Most Popular Cuisines

A bar chart was created to identify the most popular cuisines based on the number of restaurants.

### 4. Votes vs Restaurant Rating

A scatter plot was created to analyze the relationship between the number of votes and restaurant ratings.

## Key Insights

- The rating distribution shows the overall spread of restaurant ratings.
- The city analysis identifies locations with a higher concentration of restaurants.
- The cuisine analysis highlights the most popular cuisines in the dataset.
- The votes vs rating visualization helps understand whether customer engagement through votes is related to restaurant ratings.

## Conclusion

The visualizations provide a clear understanding of restaurant ratings, city-wise restaurant distribution, cuisine popularity, and the relationship between votes and ratings. These insights can help understand customer preferences and restaurant market trends.
